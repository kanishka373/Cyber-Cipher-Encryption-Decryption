🛡 About Caesar Cipher

What is Caesar Cipher?

Caesar Cipher is one of the oldest encryption techniques used in cybersecurity.

It works by shifting letters of the alphabet using a fixed key.

⚡ Example

Plain Text: HELLO

Shift Key: 3

Cipher Text: KHOOR
